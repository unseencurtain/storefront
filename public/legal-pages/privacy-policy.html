2. What data we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, delivery and billing address, phone number
  • Order data: products purchased, order value, payment method, order history
  • Account data: login credentials, wishlist and saved preferences (if you create an account)
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, cookies (see Section 6)
  • Communication data: messages you send us via the contact form, email or customer service

3. Why we process your data

  • To process and deliver your orders and handle payments
  • To manage your customer account and order history
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates and (with consent) marketing emails
  • To improve our website, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations (e.g. tax and accounting rules)

4. Legal basis

We process your data based on: performance of a contract (processing your order), legal obligation (invoicing, tax records), consent (marketing emails, non-essential cookies), and legitimate interest (fraud prevention, website security and improvement).

5. Sharing your data

We share data only where necessary with: payment providers (to process your payment), shipping carriers (to deliver your order), IT and hosting providers, and authorities where legally required. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. Cookies

Our website uses functional cookies (required for the shop and checkout to work), analytics cookies (to understand site usage) and, where consented, marketing cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences via your browser settings or our cookie banner.

7. Data retention

We retain order and invoice data for as long as required by Dutch tax law (currently 7 years) and account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of data we are not legally required to keep.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@prinscosmetic.eu. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures (such as encrypted checkout and restricted access to customer data) to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
