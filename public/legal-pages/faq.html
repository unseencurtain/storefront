Frequently Asked Questions

Orders & Shipping

How long does delivery take?
Within the Netherlands typically 1–3 business days, EU-wide 3–7 business days. See Order Tracking for details.

Do you ship internationally?
We currently ship across the EU. For deliveries outside the EU, please contact us first to check availability and shipping costs.

How can I track my order?
You'll receive a tracking link by email once your order ships, or you can check My Account > Orders.

Returns & Refunds

Can I return a product?
Yes, within 14 days of delivery, provided the item is unused and, for cosmetics/perfumes, the hygiene seal is intact. See our Refund & Returns Policy.

How long does a refund take?
Refunds are processed within 14 days of us receiving your return.

Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?
iDEAL, credit card, PayPal and Bancontact. See Payment Terms.

Are your prices including VAT?
Yes, all prices shown include applicable EU/Dutch VAT.

Products

Are your products authentic?
Yes, all fragrances and beauty products are sourced directly from the brands or authorised distributors.

Do you offer gift wrapping?
Not currently offered as standard, but let us know at checkout or via our contact form if you'd like your order gift-wrapped and we'll see what we can do.

Contact

How can I reach customer service?
Via our contact form or by emailing info@prinscosmetic.eu.
