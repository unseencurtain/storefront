Order Tracking

Want to know where your PrinsCosmetic order is? Here's how to track it.

1. Check your shipping confirmation email

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your parcel directly with the carrier.

2. Track via your account

If you placed your order while logged in, you can view your order status anytime under My Account > Orders.

4. Delivery times

  • Netherlands: 1–3 business days
  • Rest of EU: 3–7 business days
  • Other countries: please contact us for an estimated delivery time

5. Order not arrived?

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected timeframe, please contact us at info@prinscosmetic.eu with your order number and we'll look into it right away.
