Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy applies to all purchases made on prinscosmetic.eu from PrinsCosmetic, a trade name of Lovely Perfume Store B.V.

1. Right of withdrawal (14 days)

As a consumer in the EU, you have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving your goods, without giving any reason, in accordance with Dutch and EU consumer law.

2. How to return an item

  1. Contact us at info@prinscosmetic.eu within 14 days of receiving your order, stating your order number.
  2. We will confirm the return and provide return instructions/address.
  3. Send the item back within 14 days of your cancellation notice, in its original, unused and undamaged condition, with original packaging where possible.

3. Exceptions to the right of withdrawal

For hygiene and health protection reasons, the right of withdrawal does not apply to opened or unsealed cosmetics, perfumes and skincare products once the seal/hygiene packaging has been removed, unless the product is defective or damaged upon arrival.

4. Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, in their original packaging and with any hygiene seal intact to be eligible for a refund (except in case of a defect). We reserve the right to refuse a return or apply a reduced refund if the product shows signs of use beyond what is necessary to establish its nature and characteristics.

5. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the returned item or proof of return shipment, whichever is earlier.

6. Return shipping costs

Unless the item is defective or incorrect, the cost of returning the item is borne by the customer.

7. Damaged or incorrect items

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item, please contact us at info@prinscosmetic.eu within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item, and we will arrange a replacement or full refund, including return shipping costs.

8. Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. Please return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the desired product.

9. Contact

For any questions about returns or refunds, contact info@prinscosmetic.eu or write to Lovely Perfume Store B.V., Bargelaan 200, 2333 CW Leiden, The Netherlands.
